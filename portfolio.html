Iron Pulse Fitness

Premium Gym & Fitness Web Application

React / Node.js / PostgreSQL

The Business Challenge

Iron Pulse Fitness needed to digitize membership registration, scheduling, and session booking. Their legacy phone-based system caused high operational overhead and booking drop-offs.

Technical Solution

We engineered a custom scheduling platform with calendar synchronization, automated email confirmations, and database locking parameters to prevent session conflicts.

The Outcome: Reduced booking overhead by 85%, increased client registration rates by 40% in 90 days, and verified a 99% mobile page-speed index score.

MajamaMeet

High-Volume Event Ticketing Platform

HTML5 / CSS3 / Razorpay Gateway

The Business Challenge

The client required secure ticketing pages to handle rapid transaction flows and ticket delivery during peak seasonal events in Kutch, Gujarat.

Technical Solution

We designed a payment gateway integration backed by transaction logging. Every booking generates a secure QR-code ticket dynamically sent via automated scripts.

The Outcome: Processed 10,000+ tickets with 0 downtime, handled payment volumes securely, and reduced physical ticket verification queues by 75%.

MundraBloggers

High-Speed News & Content Portal

Optimized SSR / Semantic News Schema

The Business Challenge

A popular local news site struggled with slow page rendering, high layout shifts (CLS), and poor crawl indexing on Google mobile search.

Technical Solution

We created an optimized static rendering system, lazy loaded news feeds, set strict core layout dimensions, and added standard Article schema parameters.

The Outcome: Brought loading times down under 1.2 seconds, resolved layout shift issues, and saw 95% of news pages indexed and ranking on local search indices within 48 hours of posting.

ParthEnterprice

Responsive B2B Industrial Catalog Portal

HTML5 / Clean CSS3 / Catalog Search

The Business Challenge

An engineering manufacturing firm needed global lead capture channels to export heavy machinery, but lacked direct search indices for catalog specs.

Technical Solution

We developed a responsive equipment catalog with catalog query filters, instant RFQ forms, and structured PDF specs download links.

The Outcome: Generated organic B2B export leads from European and Middle-Eastern clients and boosted machinery catalog downloads by 120%.

New Akshat Fashion
