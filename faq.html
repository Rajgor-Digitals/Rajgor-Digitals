Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us

How long does it take to build a custom website?

A standard project takes between 4 to 12 weeks, depending on the scope, complexity, and rounds of feedback. We prioritize quality and thorough testing for every digital asset.

Does Rajgor Digitals provide custom e-commerce solutions?

Yes, we specialize in building scalable, secure, and conversion-focused e-commerce platforms. From inventory management to secure payment gateways, we handle it all.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes, all our websites are fully responsive and look great on all devices, from mobile phones to desktops. We follow a mobile-first indexing approach for maximum SEO benefit.

What is your payment structure?

We typically work with a 50% upfront deposit and 50% upon project completion. For larger projects, we can arrange milestone-based payments to suit your budget.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and support?

Absolutely. All our packages include post-launch support ranging from 1 to 3 months. We also offer extended maintenance plans for ongoing updates, security patches, and technical support.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, we offer complete website redesign services. We'll analyze your current site, identify areas for improvement, and create a modern, high-performing replacement while preserving your SEO rankings.

Do you offer SEO services?

Every website we build includes foundational SEO — clean code, fast loading, mobile optimization, and structured data. For advanced SEO campaigns, we can recommend trusted partners or include it in a custom package.

What technologies do you use?

We work with modern, industry-standard technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Node.js, PHP, and MySQL. We choose the best tech stack based on your project requirements.

Will I own the website after it's built?

Yes, upon final payment, you receive full ownership of the website, including all source code, design files, and content. We provide complete documentation for future reference.

Can I update the website myself after launch?

Yes, if we build your site with a CMS (Content Management System), you'll be able to update text, images, and blog posts easily. We provide training and documentation to help you manage your site.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
