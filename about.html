Why Partner with Us?

We merge programming expertise with conversion layouts to deliver high-quality digital assets.

Our founders, Shrikant Rajgor and Gaurav Rajgor, bring expert frontend and backend developer credentials to every codebase. We follow strict standards to make sure your site is crawlable, indexable, and performant:

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Core: Full metadata compliance, sitemap registration, clean markup, and preconnect optimization built directly into page structures.
  • Performance Engineering: Accelerated page loads via Canvas anim observers, compressed WebP media elements, and deferred non-critical resources.
  • Vulnerability Prevention: Implementing secure target links (using standard rel properties) and sanitizing contact routes against automated bot spam.
  • Transparent Deliverables: Client source-code ownership, direct developer consultations, and post-launch maintenance windows.
